Throng — Spawner (design_spec Chapter 13)

A synthetic stage — a floor between two walls plus hand-placed entrances — driving src/spawner.js through src/simulation.js. Entrances open at frame 35 (§13.1); the countdown (default 20) decrements every frame from 35 on (frame 35 is the first tick), so the first lemming drops at frame 54; thereafter one every (99−rate)/2+4 frames (§13.3), rotating among the entrances by released mod 4 (§13.4). Changing any parameter restarts from frame 0.
