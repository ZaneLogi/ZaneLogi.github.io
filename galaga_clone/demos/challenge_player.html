Galaga bonus-stage paths, played from bytecode

The challenging (bonus) stages — game stages 3, 7, 11, … — fly bugs through the screen instead of into formation. Each round is 5 waves; each wave fires a lefty and a righty member, and the wave-byte resolves — through the real PATH_INDEX/VARIANTS join (paths.js getChallengeWave) — to a token-free path block plus its start position. Pick a stage + wave to fly that one wave through the motion interpreter (pathrunner.js). single-path player · ← all demos
