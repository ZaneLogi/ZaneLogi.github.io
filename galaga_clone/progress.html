Faithful ports of both Z80 formation movement tasks. f_2A90: whole formation drifts ±32 px L/R at 15 Hz (1 px/4 frames). f_1DE6: per-column and per-row pixel offsets driven by d_1E64 bitmap table; rrc rotation; expand/contract counter gives ~4.2 s full cycle.
tasks/formationOscillate.js tasks/formationPulse.js state.js tasks/starfield.js
decidef_2A90 and f_1DE6 are mutually exclusive: f_2A90 runs during fly-in and attack; f_1DE6 takes over at steady state (all enemies homed, launchAttackWave off). Hand-off not yet wired — toggled manually via dev panel for now.
notef_1DE6 bitmap (d_1E64_bitmap_tables, gg1-2_fx.s:1721): 4 rows × 16 bytes. rrc each byte each tick; carry → update offset. Left cols 0–4 invert direction (expand leftward); right cols 5–9 and all 6 rows use positive direction. Counter: 0x00→0x1F expanding, jumps to 0xA0; 0xA0→0x81 contracting, jumps to 0x00.
