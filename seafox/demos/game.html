Seafox — the screen

design_spec Chapter 17. The core runs at 30 Hz and produces no picture; once per tick the renderer reads the entity list, the effects list and the session, and rebuilds color from them — background, the waterline, entities in slot order, effects, the HUD line, then any posted banner. The waterline goes down before the entities, so a torpedo crossing the surface occludes it; effects go over them, because the effects walk runs last. Nothing on this page is normative: the loop and the controls are a harness, and the simulation cannot tell it is being watched. The session cold-boots into the demo, so the opening is the same deterministic run Oracles 2 and 3 assert.
