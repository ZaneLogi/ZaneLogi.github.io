design_spec Chapter 18 — the queue, not the tone. Synthesis is free (§ 18.10) and needs ears, so nothing here listens to anything: what is normative and therefore testable is the pitch arithmetic, the eighteen sequences, one pair per tick, and the queue's two unusual properties. Nothing is ever pre-empted — a new sound is appended behind whatever is still sounding, so the audio lags the picture under load; and with no bounds test anywhere, a full ring does not drop the newest sound but either overwrites unplayed pairs in place or reads as empty and discards the whole backlog at once. Every one of § 18.6's seventeen assignments is driven from its real call site, so a later chapter that moves one is caught here.
