design_spec § 20.5, the last and most expensive oracle. The game is deterministic from cold boot until the first input, so a rendered frame at a fixed tick is reproducible to the byte. Two halves, and they are different kinds of check: anchors compare the frame against the specification — the six-colour palette, the waterline row, the HUD row — and would catch a renderer that has been consistently wrong since it was written; goldens compare it against this port's own past, and can only tell you it changed. A golden failure is a question: ask what changed and whether it was meant, and never regenerate to silence a red page — that promotes a bug to the reference. To regenerate deliberately, see the header of golden_frames.js.
