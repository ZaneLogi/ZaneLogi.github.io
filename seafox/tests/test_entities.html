Not one of the four oracles: this exists because Chapter 4 carries rules whose failures are close to undiagnosable from play. A collapsed two-phase removal silently stops a class spawning for the rest of the session; a cursor that advances after a swap silently skips an entity every time a slot is freed. Both are cheap to catch here and expensive to find later, and neither needs a walk, a handler or a renderer to exercise.
