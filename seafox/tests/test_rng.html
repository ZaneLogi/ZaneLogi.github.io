Seafox — Oracle 1: the generator

The first oracle of design_spec § 20, and the first thing in the bring-up order of § 20.8 — a pure unit test on src/core/rng.js with no simulation, no entity list and no renderer behind it. It is first because everything random in the game descends from this generator: every spawn interval, every enemy-submarine depth, the depth charge's arc, the clam's release delay, the demo's auto-fire. A generator that is subtly wrong still plays plausibly and matches no other oracle.
