Lunar Lander — flight physics on a scrolling starfield

Source-faithful flight model — a routine-level translation of Rich Moore's original (docs/research_physics.md). Real constants: gravity $11 (17), thrust TRSTAB (max $1C=28), so the craft hovers at throttle 8/15 (where thrust == gravity) and climbs above it. Gravity is Y-only; no drag (except Training friction) — true inertial coasting.

No terrain: the lander stays centred and the starfield scrolls to show motion — the arcade's own camera model (SCROLL/SCRADD). Horizontal drift scrolls the field sideways; climbing/descending scrolls it vertically. (Generated even-spread starfield; the ROM's fixed 61-point field is in starfield.html. Flame is our own design — see CLAUDE.md.)
