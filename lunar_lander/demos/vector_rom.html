Lunar Lander — vector ROM font sheet

All 26 letters + space, decoded from 034598-01.np3 (CPU $5000–$57FF) and rendered through the shared Atari DVG interpreter (dvg.js — the same chip as Asteroids). Each cell is one glyph subroutine (SVEC strokes ending in RTS), centered and uniformly scaled. The globalScale slider feeds the additive scale model (research_dvg.md §4); glyphs stay fit to their cells until SVEC saturation (scaleMode + 2 + gs > 9) starts dropping strokes.
