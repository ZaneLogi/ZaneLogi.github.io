Lunar Lander — the faithful landscape, built from the source

The lunar surface drawn exactly the way the ROM builds it (not matched from a MAME snapshot like screen.html/scroll_view.html): 24 segment-glyphs SEG001-024 compose into 16 sections SECT01-16, ordered by the LNMIN table and vertically placed by MINTBL. SECT11 is the flat SEG019 pad. The surface wraps — section 16 ends exactly where section 01 began (blue dots) — so it loops seamlessly as you scroll. Full decode in docs/research_vector_usage.md §3. Terrain only — no lander, no HUD. This wide overview is the zoom-out (major-scape) view — the small-lander view you fly in most of the time; the close-up landing terrain is the same surface zoomed in.

—

zoom 1× shows the whole 4096-wide loop at once; zoom in and scroll (or auto-scroll) to traverse it — past the right edge the surface wraps back to the start. The faithful ✓ flag means every section boundary landed on its MINTBL value and the loop closed.
