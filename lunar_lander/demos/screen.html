Lunar Lander — in-game screen (1024×768)

The gameplay screen composited on a faithful 1024×768 DVG field (origin bottom-left, Y-up): the $5458 HUD label grid (byte-faithful, as hud.html) with formatted values, the terrain with multiplier-labelled landing pads, and the lander. HUD value layout + pad multipliers were measured from a MAME gameplay frame (2026-06-29): left column left-aligned at x=204, right column right-aligned to x=878, and pads carry 1+64/width multipliers (16→5X / 32→3X / 64→2X). The terrain tile sequence + on-screen scale remain the address-order approximation (CPU-side). See scroll_view.html for the horizontal-scroll/wrap.
