Lunar Lander — vector-ROM demos

Faithful byte-decodes of Atari Lunar Lander (1979), which drives the same Atari DVG as Asteroids. Most pages render shapes straight from the picture ROMs through dvg.js; the flight-physics and crash-explosion demos are source-faithful translations now that the original program source is in hand. Pick one:

Flight physics 🚀

Fly the lander on a scrolling starfield with the real source physics (gravity $11, hover at throttle 8) — a routine-level translation of Rich Moore's original. Four difficulty modes; the craft stays centred, the world scrolls.

demos/physics.html · A34573.1A source
Crash explosion 💥

The lander blows up — a routine-level port of the BOOM routine: a tumbling cabin octagon + 6 fragments fly out and wink out over INDEX 1→127, then replay with a new random 1-of-4 pattern. Pattern picker, zoom, INDEX scrub.

demos/explosion.html · A34573.1A + 034599
Faithful landscape ⛰️

The lunar surface built the way the ROM builds it — 16 sections in LNMIN order, placed by MINTBL, wrapping seamlessly. Terrain only, no lander/HUD. The source-driven counterpart to the MAME-matched screen demos.

demos/landscape.html · 034598 + LNMIN/MINTBL
360° rotation ↻

The lander spun through a full circle (9-pose quadrant + X/Y mirror), shown at both ROM sizes, plus a globalScale size demo.

demos/rotation.html · 034599
Thrust flame 🔥

The lander tilted across its gameplay range (head left → up → right, ±90°, no upside-down) with a throttle-driven thrust flame — drawn programmatically (no flame glyph exists in ROM; our own design), modelled on Asteroids + Seb's Moon Lander.
